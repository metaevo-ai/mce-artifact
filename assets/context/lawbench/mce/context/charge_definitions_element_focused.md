# Charge Definitions (Element-Focused)

## Property Crimes

### 盗窃
- **Elements**: (1) Secret taking, (2) Without victim's awareness, (3) Intent to permanently deprive
- **Key Question**: Was the victim unaware of the taking?
- **Common Confusion**: NOT deception (诈骗), NOT position-based (职务侵占)

### 诈骗
- **Elements**: (1) Deception, (2) Victim voluntarily transfers property, (3) Intent to deprive
- **Key Question**: Did victim voluntarily transfer property due to deception?
- **Important**: Deception must be the direct cause of victim's "willing" transfer

### 非法吸收公众存款
- **Elements**: (1) Solicit deposits, (2) From UNSPECIFIC/PUBLIC, (3) Promise returns, (4) Without authorization
- **Key Distinction from 诈骗**:
  - 非法吸收公众存款: "面向不特定公众" (面向不特��公众), promise returns, usually multiple investors
  - 诈骗: Deceive SPECIFIC individuals, false reasons for obtaining money
- **Key Words to Identify**: "以...名义", "承诺支付月利息", multiple lenders (13人, 7人等)

### 职务侵占
- **Elements**: (1) Actor in position of trust, (2) Property in actor's custody, (3) Intent to permanently deprive
- **Key Question**: Was the actor already entrusted with the property?

## Drug Crimes

### 容留他人吸毒
- **Elements**: (1) Provide LOCATION/场所, (2) For OTHERS to use drugs
- **Key Distinction from 吸毒**:
  - 容留他人吸毒: YOU provide the location, OTHERS use drugs there
  - Simple drug use: Just using drugs yourself = no separate crime (吸毒 only)
- **COMMON ERROR**: "容留" means "providing hospitality/accommodation" - not just being present
- **Example of CORRECT identification**: "在被告人房间内容留他人吸毒" = 容留他人吸毒
- **Example of WRONG identification**: "被告人与他人一起吸毒" = NOT 容留他人吸毒

### 制造毒品
- **Elements**: (1) Production, (2) Of illegal narcotics
- **Key**: Even small-scale production (13.97g + 11476g liquid) = 制造毒品

### 持有、使用假币
- **Elements**: (1) Possession AND/OR use, (2) Of counterfeit currency, (3) To obtain goods/services
- **Key Distinction from 诈骗**:
  - 持有、使用假币: Use fake money to buy things = this charge
  - 诈骗: Use deception to obtain money/property = 诈骗
- **Key Pattern**: "使用假币购买..." = 持有、使用假币

## Position Crimes

### 受贿
- **Elements**: (1) State official, (2) Using position, (3) Accepting property/gifts, (4) For improper benefits
- **Key Question**: Is the actor a state official using their position?

### 行贿
- **Elements**: (1) Give property/gifts, (2) To state official, (3) For improper benefits
- **Key Question**: Was the giving to a state official for improper benefits?

### 单位行贿
- **Elements**: (1) Conducted in name of unit, (2) For unit's benefit, (3) Bribery behavior
- **Key Distinction from 行贿**:
  - 行贿: Personal bribery
  - 单位行贿: Bribery for company/unit benefit

### 对非国家工作人员行贿
- **Elements**: (1) Give property, (2) To NON-STATE personnel, (3) For commercial benefits
- **Key Distinction**: Recipient is NOT a state official (e.g., hospital department head in commercial context)

---

## Document Crimes

### 伪造、变造、买卖国家机关公文、证件、印章
- **Elements**: (1) Falsify/make/sell, (2) Government documents/seals
- **CRITICAL**: Must use COMPLETE official name - includes "公文" AND "证件" AND "印章"
- **Example**: Selling fake "流动人口婚育证明" = this charge

### 虚开增值税专用发票、用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款发票
- **Elements**: (1) Issue false VAT invoices, (2) For export tax rebates or tax deductions
- **CRITICAL**: Must use COMPLETE official name (cannot abbreviate to just "虚开增值税专用发票")
- **Key Pattern**: "在没有实际货物交易的情况下开具增值税专用发票"

### 侵犯公民个人信息
- **Elements**: (1) Illegally obtain/collect/provide/sell, (2) Citizens' personal information
- **Key Pattern**: "窃取快递单据上面的个人信息" = this charge
- **Note**: "非法获取公民个人信息" is the same charge (both are valid)

---

## Violence Crimes

### 故意伤害
- **Elements**: (1) Intentional harm, (2) Specific victim, (3) Injury result
- **Key Question**: Was the victim specifically targeted with intent to harm?

### 聚众斗殴
- **Elements**: (1) Gathering crowd, (2) Mutual fighting
- **Key Distinction from 故意伤害**:
  - 聚众斗殴: Mutual combat between groups
  - 故意伤害: Targeted attack on victim
- **Note**: 聚众斗殴致人重伤 → transforms to 故意伤害 (not both)

### 虐待被监管人
- **Elements**: (1)监狱等监管机构工作人员, (2)对被监管人实施殴打或体罚虐待
- **Key**: Only applies to prison/correctional facility staff

---

## Financial & Economic Crimes

### 逃税
- **Elements**: (1) Evade tax payment, (2) Through concealment/non-declaration, (3) Large amount + >10% of tax owed
- **Key Pattern**: "应申报纳税而未申报纳税"

### 集资诈骗
- **Elements**: (1) Collect funds from public, (2) Using fraud methods, (3) Intent to illegally possess
- **Key Distinction from 非法吸收公众存款**:
  - 集资诈骗: Intent to illegally possess funds from the start
  - 非法吸收公众存款: May intend to return funds (but illegal)

### 倒卖车票、船票
- **Elements**: (1) Resell train/ship tickets, (2) For profit, (3) Without authorization
- **CRITICAL**: Must include BOTH "车票" AND "船票" in the name

---

## Other Common Charges

### 诬告陷害
- **Elements**: (1) Fabricate crime facts, (2) Accuse innocent person, (3) Intent to make them criminally investigated
- **Key Pattern**: "捏造...参与盗窃的事实，使...受到刑事拘留"

### 非法处置查封、扣押、冻结的财产
- **Elements**: (1) Dispose/hide/transfer, (2) Court-seized/seized/frozen property
- **Key Pattern**: "私自将法院扣押的轿车开走"

### 走私珍贵动物、珍贵动物制品
- **Elements**: (1) Smuggle, (2) Endangered wildlife or products
- **CRITICAL**: Must include BOTH "珍贵动物" AND "珍贵动物制品"

### 强迫卖淫
- **Elements**: (1) Force/coerce, (2) Someone to engage in prostitution
- **Key**: Often appears with "组织、引诱、容留、介绍卖淫" in combination charges
